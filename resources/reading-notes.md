# Course readings

These references were consulted in the local course archive during the original study. The complete course books are not required by the program and were not duplicated here. Bibliographic notes remain available without depending on sibling folders.

## Markowitz

Markowitz, Harry. 1952. “Portfolio Selection.” *The Journal of Finance* 7(1):77–91. DOI: [10.1111/j.1540-6261.1952.tb01525.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1952.tb01525.x).

The introduction and mean–variance framework were inspected. Weights, expected returns and covariances define the estimated frontier; it does not guarantee future performance.

## Ross

Ross, Sheldon M. 2011. *An Elementary Introduction to Mathematical Finance*. 3rd ed. Cambridge University Press. Chapter 9, “Valuing by Expected Utility,” pp. 165–192. ISBN 978-0521192538.

Required reading in the Module 6 Overview. Utility, risk aversion and portfolio-selection passages were consulted, mainly pp. 166–176. The study does not estimate the investor's personal utility function.
